## What I Found

The final interpretation remains a two-error operating-point result. Under the byte-identical common prompt, Qwen reduced plain and pressured unsafe-compliance failure by 55.0 and 40.1 percentage points relative to Mistral while increasing authorized-control refusal by 18.8 points. No model is uniformly safer without a declared loss function. Prompt effects are generator-specific, quiet authorization gaps can be harder than conspicuous pressure, and the only observed mitigation pass does not survive combined measurement stress.

## What Remains Open

Measurement validity is the principal limitation because outcomes are panel-judged rather than independently human-labeled. External validity is also unresolved: the bank is synthetic and text-only, with two quantized generators and no sensing, actuation, operator behavior, timing, or field prevalence. The next discriminating study should use blinded human adjudication, three generators, matched authorized controls, calibrated defer outcomes, counterbalanced salience and pressure, and a sealed held-out scenario set.
